Breakfast at AM

Breakfast in Playa del Carmen, on Calle 38

  • Since January 2026
  • Parking Plenty of street parking (metered).
  • Pet friendly Your dog is welcome
  • Delivery We don't offer delivery yet; coming soon.

Since January 2026, AM by Mu has been serving breakfast and brunch on Calle 38 in Playa del Carmen, between 5th Avenue and Calle Flamingos. We're open 8 am to 3 pm every day except Tuesday, and the full menu is served all day.

For breakfast there are green poblano or red morita chilaquiles, motuleño-style chilaquiles, eggs your way or ahogados, eggs & steak with Angus skirt steak, omelettes, burrata toast on sourdough and three pain perdu: the classic with salted caramel, blueberry & crème and banana brûlée. On weekdays before 11 am, AM Express is a full breakfast for $149.

To go with it: specialty coffee, bottomless for $75 with your brunch, ceremonial Uji matcha lattes, hojicha, fresh juices and mimosas.

There are vegetarian options —chilaquiles, huevos ahogados, sweet corn hotcake, pain perdu and burrata toast— and a vegan one: flautas ahogadas filled with plant-based meat. We're pet friendly, too: your dog is welcome.

We're half a block from 5th Avenue, in Zazil-Ha, with plenty of metered street parking. Come solo with your laptop, with friends, with the family —there's a terrace and high chairs— or after a run with the Run Club.

FAQ

What time do you serve breakfast?

Every day except Tuesday, 8 am to 3 pm. The full breakfast and brunch menu is served all day.

Do you take reservations?

Yes, on WhatsApp at +52 984 385 6781.

Do you deliver?

We don't offer delivery yet; coming soon.

Is there parking?

Yes, there's plenty of metered street parking.

Can I bring my pet?

Yes, we're pet friendly.

Do you have vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes. For vegans, the flautas ahogadas filled with plant-based meat. Vegetarian: chilaquiles, huevos ahogados, sweet corn hotcake, pain perdu, burrata toast, croissants and the fruit bowl.

How do I get an invoice (factura)?

Ask the captain at the restaurant.

Do you have gluten-free options?

Yes: the sweet corn hotcake is gluten free, and every coffee can be made with plant-based milk.

Do you have bottomless drinks?

Yes: bottomless orange & grapefruit mimosas, Aperol spritz, Fluffy garibaldi and affogato carajillo for $399 MXN per person (1.5 h), plus bottomless coffee for $75 MXN with your brunch.

Where are you?

Calle 38 between 5th Avenue and Calle Flamingos, Zazil-Ha, Playa del Carmen, half a block from 5th Avenue.
