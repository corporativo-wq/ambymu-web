FAQ

What are your opening hours?

We're open 8 am to 3 pm every day except Tuesday.

Do you have bottomless coffee?

Yes. With any brunch, bottomless coffee is $75 MXN per person (1.5 h max): espresso, flat white, latte, cappuccino and americano.

Do you have vegan or gluten-free options?

Yes: vegan flautas ahogadas, Beyond Meat burger, gluten-free sweet corn hotcake and plant-based milk for every coffee.

Where are you?

Calle 38 between 5th Avenue and Calle Flamingos, Zazil-Ha, Playa del Carmen.

Do you have bottomless mimosas?

Yes, bottomless mimosas and Aperol spritz for $399 MXN.
